# Exam Cram Bangla (`exam-cram-bangla`)

An ultra-high-retention, zero-fluff exam preparation skill designed for engineering and technical subjects under strict exam time limits (e.g., 1-hour total revision, ~3 minutes per question).

---

## 🎯 Standing Principles & Rules

### 1. Mandatory First Step — প্রশ্ন আসলে কী চাচ্ছে?
- **ALWAYS START** by explaining in 1–2 direct, plain Bangla sentences what the examiner is actually demanding.
- Clarify the core question requirements before presenting any solutions or theories.

### 2. Time-Constrained & Zero Fluff (3-Minute Rule):
- The reader typically has only **2–3 minutes per question**.
- **Do not exaggerate or write overly long paragraphs.** No unnecessary history or filler text.
- Provide the essential intuition first, followed immediately by high-scoring exam points and exact numbers/units.

### 3. Language Formula:
- **Explanations & Intuition:** Simple, conversational, everyday Bangla (সহজ বাংলা).
- **Technical Content:** Keywords, parameters, equations, units, test numbers, and limits MUST remain in **English** (bolded for instant recognition).

### 4. Meaningful Real-Word Mnemonics (CRITICAL RULE):
- **NEVER create random non-word letter sequences** (e.g., `CTHAI`, `XQPL` are strictly forbidden).
- The mnemonic **MUST BE AN ACTUAL, MEANINGFUL ENGLISH WORD** (e.g., `SPLIT`, `STAND`, `GLIDE`, `CANT`, `PACT`, `WASH`, `CURE`, `FACTS`).
- **Question-to-Mnemonic Hook:** Explicitly explain *why and how* reading the question in the exam hall triggers this mnemonic word.
- **Strict 3-Column Table Format:**
  ```markdown
  | Letter | English Keyword / Point | সহজ বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা |
  | :---: | :--- | :--- |
  ```

## 📋 Standard Master Output Structure

For every question or topic, strictly use this clean 4-part structure:

### ১. ❓ প্রশ্ন আসলে কী চাচ্ছে? (What the question wants)
- পরীক্ষক এই প্রশ্নে ঠিক কী কী নির্দিষ্ট উত্তর দেখতে চান—সেটি ১-২ লাইনে একদম স্পষ্ট করে বলে দেওয়া।

### ২. 💡 সহজ ভাষায় কনসেপ্ট বোঝা (Intuitive Understanding First)
- বাস্তব উদাহরণ, অ্যানালজি বা মেকানিজম দিয়ে পুরো কনসেপ্টটি সহজে বুঝিয়ে দেওয়া, যাতে মুখস্থ করার আগেই বিষয়টি পুরোপুরি মাথায় ঢুকে যায়।
- প্রয়োজনীয় টেকনিক্যাল সংজ্ঞা, লিমিট ও মান পরিষ্কারভাবে উপস্থাপন করা।

### ৩. 🧠 পরীক্ষার জন্য মুখস্থ রাখার ট্রিক (Mnemonic Table)
- **Mnemonic Word:** [A REAL English Word]

- **৩-কলামের টেবিল:**
  | Letter | English Keyword / Point | সহজ বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা |
  | :---: | :--- | :--- |

### ৪. 📌 পরীক্ষার খাতায় লেখার ছক / পয়েন্ট (Exam-Ready Summary)
- পরীক্ষার খাতায় সরাসরি ফুল মার্কস পাওয়ার মতো পরিষ্কার টেবিল বা পয়েন্ট, যা দ্রুত রিভিশন দেওয়া যায়।

## 📚 Real-World Master Library of Mnemonics (Battle-Tested)

### Transportation & Railway Engineering:
- **Railway Gauge Non-uniformity Difficulties:** `SPLIT` (Stock incompatibility, Passenger delay, Loss & theft, Inefficiency in emergencies, Terminal duplication)
- **Flat-Footed Rail Advantages:** `STAND` (Stability lateral, Transmission uniform, Area-strength efficiency, No chairs required, Direct economy)
- **Functions of Rails:** `GLIDE` (Guidance lateral, Level surface, Immense stress bearing, Dispersion of load, Energy-saving low friction)
- **Why Cant Deficiency is Provided:** `CANT` (Conflict at turnouts, Avoid overturning slow trains, No severe speed crawling, Tolerable passenger comfort)
- **Marshall vs Hveem Differences:** `PACT` (Preparation/Compaction, Asphalt content start, Criteria for design, Tests conducted)
- **Affinity (Hydrophilic vs Hydrophobic):** `WASH` (Water affinity, Acidity & nature, Stripping risk, Hard-rock examples)
- **Cutback Asphalt (RC, MC, SC) & Advantages:** `CURE` (Cutback concept, Uses in pavement, Residual bitumen >80%, Evaporation & solvents)
- **HMA Aggregate Properties (Cubical Shape):** `FACTS` (Flat & elongated limits, Angularity coarse & fine, Clay content, Toughness, Soundness)

### Pavement Design & Testing (Classic Library):
- **AASHO Road Test Summary:** `PILOT` (Purpose, Interim Guide, Loads, Ottawa, Test Loops)
- **Major Outcomes of AASHO:** `STEPS` (Serviceability PSI, Traffic ESAL, Empirical, Pavement simplified, Structural number SN)
- **Why AASHTO Fails in BD:** `CALM` (Climate, Alluvial soil, Loading up to 400M, Materials brick)
- **Evolution of ESAL:** `CHATS` (CBR, Hveem, AASHO, Texas, Scrivner)
- **4th Power Law:** `LEAP` (LEF, Exponent 4, Axle load P, Pivot 18 kip)
- **Flexible Pavement Methods:** `TAMED` (TRL 31, Asphalt Institute, Mechanistic-Empirical, Empirical AASHTO, Design Catalog)
- **Rigid Pavement Methods:** `TAP` (TRL 29, AASHTO 1993, PCA)
- **Pavement Design Objectives:** `TARGET` (Total thickness, All individual layers, Reinforcement, Guaranteed, Expected traffic, Time life)
- **Why Pavement Design is Complex:** `TEST` (Traffic repetitive, Environment exposed, Soil variability, Trouble forecasting)
- **Design Considerations:** `RESPECT` (Roadbed, Environment, Shoulder, Performance, Economics, Construction materials, Traffic)
- **Why Joints are Used:** `MOVES` (Movements, Organized cracks, Void infiltration limit, Economy in steel, Shear load transfer)
- **Contraction vs Construction Joint:** `HELP` (How made, End of pour, Load transfer, Placement)
- **Why Contraction Joint is Better:** `GRIP` (Grip aggregate interlock, Resistance to faulting, Integrated monolithic, Pour age identical)
- **Dowel vs Tie Bar:** `TWINS` (Transverse vs Long., Weight transfer vs Tie together, Iron surface smooth vs deformed, Numeric size 25-32 vs 12-19mm, Standard 2ft vs Bond)
- **Joint-wise Rigid Pavement (JPCP, JRCP, CRCP):** `STEEL` (Slab length, Traffic level, Extent of steel, Elimination of joints, Load transfer)
